# Huong dan su dung Chino's Warehouse

## App dung de lam gi?
App giup chu cua hang va nhan vien:
- Tim nhanh san pham trong kho
- Biet san pham dang nam o zone nao, tang nao
- Xem ton kho theo so thung
- Theo doi han su dung va canh bao sap het hang

## Y nghia mau cham (xanh/vang/cam/do)
Mau cham duoc tinh tu ca **han su dung** va **so thung con lai**:

- **Do**: da het han hoac da het hang (`0 thung`)
- **Cam**: con rat it thung (<= nguong "con rat it")
- **Vang**: sap het han hoac sap het hang (<= nguong "sap het")
- **Xanh**: binh thuong

## Cach tim san pham
1. Vao tab `Kho`
2. Go ten san pham o o tim kiem
3. App hien ket qua va danh dau zone co san pham bang cham mau
4. Cham vao san pham de mo man hinh chi tiet

## Cach them lo hang moi
1. Mo zone can them hang (trong map) hoac vao man hinh chi tiet san pham
2. Bam `Them hang` / `Them lo hang moi`
3. Chon zone, tang, san pham
4. Nhap so thung, han su dung, ghi chu
5. Bam `Luu`

Neu trung lot (cung san pham + zone + tang + han), app se hoi co cong don so thung hay khong.

## Cach sua / xoa / chuyen lo hang
- **Sua lo**: vao chi tiet san pham hoac zone detail, bam lot de sua thong tin
- **Xoa lo**: bam nut xoa (thung rac) tai lot
- **Chuyen lo**: bam nut mui ten, chon zone dich va tang dich

## Cach doc tab Thong bao
Moi card thong bao co the co toi da 2 badge (chi hien khi co van de):

- Badge han:
  - `Da het han` (do)
  - `Sap het han` (vang)
- Badge thung:
  - `Da het hang` (do)
  - `Con rat it` (do)
  - `Sap het hang` (vang)

Neu khong co badge nao thi san pham dang o trang thai binh thuong.

## Cach doi nguong canh bao ngay va thung
1. Mo `Chi tiet san pham`
2. O phan thong tin san pham (ngay duoi ten/barcode), cap nhat:
   - `Nguong canh bao (ngay)`
   - `Nguong canh bao sap het (thung)`
   - `Nguong canh bao con rat it (thung)`
3. Bam `Luu` o tung dong

Sau khi luu, thong bao va mau cham cua san pham se cap nhat theo nguong moi tren map, products va notifications.
